This site records the work undertaken at Shenton Park Observatory.

The current focus is spectroscopy, including observations contributed to collaborative research projects. The Journal Archive contains earlier observing notes, equipment changes and software development, kept mainly as a personal record. Historical astronomical images and published results are also collected here.
